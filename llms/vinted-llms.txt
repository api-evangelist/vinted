# Vinted

> Vinted is a European consumer-to-consumer (C2C) marketplace for buying and selling secondhand clothes, shoes, and accessories, operating across web and mobile apps in many markets. Vinted does not publish a public developer API, SDKs, or a developer portal; this profile captures the company's public identity, legal, and security surfaces only.
